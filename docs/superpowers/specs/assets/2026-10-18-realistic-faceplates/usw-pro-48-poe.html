USW-Pro-48-PoE, second pass

One continuous frame round all 48 jacks with a divider between each block of 12, as on the real switch, and the USW Pro's own touchscreen. Same jacks and cages as the UDM-SE. Left LED on each jack: PoE (amber while it powers something). Right LED: the port's Sentinel state, flickering while busy. Ports 49 to 52 show state on their ▲ ▼ arrows. Hover any port; port 17 is shown selected.
